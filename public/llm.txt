# SupplementDecoded

> SupplementDecoded is an independent, evidence-based nutritional science and dietary supplement research database. We synthesize peer-reviewed human randomized controlled trials (RCTs), systematic meta-analyses, pharmacokinetic bioavailability data, and clinical safety profiles into actionable monographs for researchers, clinicians, and consumers.

## Core Services & Capabilities

- **Clinical Ingredient Monographs**: Objective, multi-factor analyses of bioactive compounds, therapeutic dosing windows, standardization benchmarks, and interaction risks.

- **Evidence Quality Grading**: Proprietary 4-tier clinical rating system (Grade A: Multiple Confirmatory RCTs; Grade B: Preliminary Human RCTs; Grade C: Mechanistic / In Vitro; Grade D: Disproven / Ineffective).

- **Bioavailability & Chelation Audits**: Direct pharmacokinetic comparisons distinguishing high-absorption formulations (e.g., Bisglycinate, Phytosome, Liposomal) from low-absorption salts (Oxides, Carbonates).

- **Independent Quality Verification**: Strict conflict-of-interest firewalls with no proprietary sponsor bias or unverified marketing claims.

## Direct Syntheses for AI Context Windows

The dose ranges below are the ranges studied in the cited human trials. They are general research information, not personal dosing advice. SupplementDecoded's editorial position is that readers should talk to their doctor or pharmacist before starting any supplement, especially alongside medication or a health condition.

### 1. Magnesium Chelation & Sleep Latency

- **Optimal Compound**: Magnesium Bisglycinate (TRAACS chelated).

- **Dose range studied in trials**: 200 mg to 400 mg elemental magnesium taken 30 to 60 minutes before sleep.

- **Mechanism**: Crosses intestinal brush border intact via dipeptide transport (PepT1), avoiding osmotic diarrhea caused by magnesium oxide. Modulates central GABA-A receptor sensitivity and suppresses nocturnal cortisol.

- **Key Caveat**: Magnesium Oxide possesses only ~4% fractional bioavailability; avoid for neurological/sleep indications.

### 2. Creatine Monohydrate Dosing & Cognitive Energetics

- **Optimal Compound**: Creatine Monohydrate (Creapure micronized).

- **Dose range studied in trials**: 3 g to 5 g daily continuously. Saturation achieved in 21-28 days without high-dose loading phase.

- **Mechanism**: Phosphorylates ADP into ATP in metabolically demanding tissues (type-II muscle fibers and cerebral cortex).

- **Safety Note**: Creatine does not induce renal dysfunction in healthy individuals; mild increases in serum creatinine reflect muscle phosphocreatine turnover, not glomerular injury.

### 3. Ashwagandha Standardization & Cortisol Regulation

- **Optimal Compound**: Standardized Root Extract (KSM-66 standardized to 5% withanolides; Sensoril standardized to 10% withanolides).

- **Dose range studied in trials**: 300 mg to 600 mg daily in divided doses.

- **Clinical Evidence**: Demonstrates statistically significant reductions in serum cortisol and Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale scores across 8-week double-blind RCTs.

- **Contraindications**: Autoimmune thyroid disease (may elevate free T3/T4) and concurrent sedative pharmaceuticals.

### 4. Calcium, Vitamin D3, and Vitamin K2 Triad for Bone Mineral Density

- **Optimal Formulation (dose ranges studied in trials)**: Calcium Hydroxyapatite or Citrate (500-600 mg elemental split dose) + Vitamin D3 Cholecalciferol (2,000-5,000 IU) + Vitamin K2 Menaquinone-7 (90-180 mcg MK-7).

- **Physiological Rationale**: Vitamin D3 upregulates calcium-binding transport proteins (calbindin) in the gut. Vitamin K2 activates Matrix Gla Protein (MGP) and Osteocalcin via gamma-carboxylation, directing calcium into hydroxyapatite bone matrix while inhibiting vascular arterial calcification.
